## Visual specification / reconstruction prompt

Create a clean, premium, playful 3D glossy wordmark logo reading **“CUM.G”** on a fully transparent background. The composition is horizontal and tightly framed. Use very thick, rounded, inflated bubble-letter typography with smooth bevels, soft volumetric depth, polished plastic/candy material, strong specular highlights, subtle ambient occlusion and realistic soft reflections.

The letters **CUM** are predominantly pearlescent ivory / warm off-white with a very light blush tint, edged and shadowed by saturated hot-pink/magenta reflections. After the M, place a large glossy spherical magenta-pink dot at baseline height. Immediately after that dot, place the word **PONTO** vertically in compact uppercase letters, stacked top-to-bottom between the M/dot area and the final G; the stacked letters are glossy saturated neon pink/magenta with dark inner extrusion/shadows.

The final **G** is oversized and visually dominant, sculpted like a continuous viscous/liquid candy form. It transitions through coral, hot pink, fuchsia and deep magenta/purple toward the lower curl. The G should have an exaggerated spiral lower terminal and a fluid splash-like upper terminal ending in a rounded droplet. Add bright white elongated reflection streaks along the upper/right surfaces to emphasize the glassy, gel-like 3D material.

Keep the silhouette friendly, bold and memorable; no sharp corners, no thin strokes. Use studio-quality product lighting, high-end 3D render finish, crisp antialiased edges, strong depth but no separate cast shadow background. Preserve transparency outside the logo. No extra icons, scenery, mockup surface, border, watermark, slogan or additional text.

### Layout lock
- Exact visible wording/composition: `CUM` + large dot + vertically stacked `PONTO` + oversized `G`.
- `CUM` occupies roughly the left 55–60% of the composition.
- Vertical `PONTO` acts as a narrow bridge before the final G.
- Final G occupies roughly the right 30–35% and is taller than CUM.
- Maintain generous internal counters and legibility despite the inflated forms.

### Material / lighting lock
- CUM: pearl ivory glossy plastic with blush/pink edge bounce.
- Dot + PONTO: hot-pink/fuchsia glossy plastic.
- G: liquid-gradient glossy gel, coral → pink → fuchsia → purple.
- High-gloss reflections, white studio highlight streaks, soft dark-magenta extrusion/occlusion.
- Transparent canvas.

### Negative constraints
Do not use flat vector styling, matte material, metallic chrome, realistic bodily anatomy, photographic people, explicit imagery, background scenes, texture noise, grain, typography substitutions, misspelled letters, extra punctuation, or detached decorative elements.
